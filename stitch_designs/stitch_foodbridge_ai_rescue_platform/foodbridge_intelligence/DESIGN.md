## Brand & Style

This design system sets the visual standard for an ultra-premium AI food rescue operations and social impact intelligence platform. It balances mission-driven civic purpose with mission-critical enterprise engineering. The emotional tone is authoritative, analytical, and reassuring—transforming volatile logistics, supply anomalies, and surplus expiry variables into clear, actionable intelligence.

The visual direction merges **Modern Clean Editorial** with **Precision Data Density**:
- Crisp structured interfaces that treat perishability timers, cold-chain audits, and predictive routing with the precision of a quantitative trading platform.
- Calm, organic micro-details that root the software in tangible nourishment, social stewardship, and real-world supply networks.
- Strict visual hierarchy designed to eradicate cognitive friction during high-stress dispatch windows and time-sensitive rescue allocations.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for tactical operations under dynamic warehouse, transit, and desk illumination. It anchors on a deep botanical teal and lively mint accents, balanced with assertive telemetry markers.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Emerald Sprout - `#0F766E`)**: Anchors primary workflows, brand touchpoints, critical navigation elements, and primary call-to-actions. Conveys institutional trust, biological freshness, and logistical control.
- **Secondary (Bright Mint - `#14B8A6`)**: AI route optimizations, predictive trend highlights, automated match triggers, and energetic micro-interactions.
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - **Harvest Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: Ambient warning states, weather routing disruptions, and perishable items within 12–24 hours of optimal distribution.
  - **Rescue Coral (`#E11D48`)**: High-risk expiry hazards, cold-chain temperature breaches, expired SLA flags, and urgent vehicle breakdowns.
  - **Low-Waste Mint (`#10B981`)**: Confirmed rescue transfers, balanced supply tallies, validated temperature logs, and positive impact certifications.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals**:
  - **Canvas Surface (`#F8FAFC`)**: Crisp Porcelain background offering high contrast against cards without ocular fatigue.
  - **Elevated Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure White card layers providing clean separation.
  - **Inverted / Deep Operations (`#0F172A`)**: Obsidian Kitchen for low-light terminal headers, metrics banners, and route telemetry panels.
  - **Border Stroke (`#E2E8F0`)**: Slate micro-lines maintaining structure without visual heaviness.
  - **Text Hierarchy**: Deep Charcoal (`#0F172A`) for primary editorial headers and data readouts; Neutral Slate (`#64748B`) for units, secondary labels, and logistical metadata.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent metrics, card headers, and navigation anchors, with the neutral readability of **Inter** for high-density dispatch logs, telemetry rows, and tabular data.

### Numerical Data Formatting & Legibility
All dynamic figures—including route times, ambient temperatures, weight calculations (kg/lbs), and matching confidence indices—must render with tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent optical jitter across live dashboards. Section labels and tabular column headings should use `label-sm` set in uppercase to retain clarity at compact viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform follows a responsive 12-column fluid grid system across desktop, scaling down to 8 columns on tablet viewports and 4 columns on mobile handsets.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (1280px and above)**: Full 12-column grid. Outer canvas margin is pinned to `space-xl` (2.5rem) with continuous `gutter` (1.5rem). High-density metric panels occupy spans of 3 or 4 columns; interactive routing maps occupy spans of 8 or 12.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout. Metric cards shift to pairs of 4-column spans. Margins condense to `margin` (2rem).
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column fluid flow. Margin and gutters drop to `margin-mobile` (1rem) and `gutter-mobile` (0.75rem) to maximize screen area for live driver manifests and incident alerts.

Dense tabular tables and parameter clusters utilize `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) gaps. Module padding inside operational cards uses `space-md` (16px) on mobile and `space-lg` (24px) on desktop interfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined combination of **micro-borders** and **tinted ambient shadows**, avoiding heavy dropshadows or muddy overlays.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8FAFC` (Crisp Porcelain). Non-interactive, grounding backdrop.
2. **Level 1 (Standard Card / Resting Component)**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) backed by a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0` and an ambient shadow tinted with the primary hue: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.03)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover / Active Routing Elements)**: Slight elevation with a subtle secondary tint: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 10px 20px -2px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.08)`, border shifts to `rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.3)`.
4. **Level 3 (Floating Dispatch Drawers, Overlays & Flyouts)**: Elevated above canvas layers: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`, border maintained in `#E2E8F0`.
5. **Level 4 (Inverted Dark Terminals)**: `#0F172A` with an internal 1px highlight border `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` and exterior ambient wash: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a coordinated radius hierarchy anchored on a base token value of `2` (0.5rem / 8px):
- **Base UI Elements (`rounded`)**: 8px (`0.5rem`) for standard inputs, segmented controls, dropdown triggers, and tabular action cells.
- **Large Cards & Editorial Modules (`rounded-lg`)**: 16px (`1.0rem` / `rounded-2xl`) for intelligence dashboards, dispatch route maps, and hero metric clusters.
- **Floating Modals & Dispatch Sliders (`rounded-xl`)**: 24px (`1.5rem`) for heavy flyout surfaces and root app framing.
- **Telemetry Chips & Badges (`rounded-full`)**: Fully pill-shaped (9999px) for operational state indicators, live countdowns, and confidence score tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0F766E` with `#FFFFFF` text. Height 40px (desktop) / 44px (touch). Padding: 0 16px. Border-radius: 8px. Hover shifts to `#115E59` with an ambient glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary / Soft**: `#FFFFFF` surface with `#0F766E` text and a 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Hover changes border to `#14B8A6` and background to `rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.04)`.
- **Tertiary / Destructive Action**: Solid `#E11D48` for emergency rescue cancels; subtle soft pink (`rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.08)`) with `#E11D48` text for low-priority alerts.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- Cards are constructed using `#FFFFFF` with an exact 16px (`rounded-2xl`) corner radius and a 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Header bands are compact: 48px height with title (`headline-sm`) aligned left and live status tags aligned right.
- High-priority operational metrics pair a numeric display (`data-display`) with an uppercase metadata label (`label-sm`).

### Chips & Trust Badges
- Constructed with pill geometry (9999px radius), 24px height, and `label-sm` type.
- **Success (Impact Certified / Recovered)**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`. Include a 6px static status dot.
- **Warning (Perish Risk / Bottleneck)**: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`.
- **Critical (Cold-Chain Hazard)**: Background `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.1)`, text `#BE123C`, border `1px solid rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.2)`. Includes a pulse animation on the dot.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height 40px, 8px corner radius, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#CBD5E1` border.
- Text set in `body-md` (`#0F172A`), placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state removes default browser rings and applies a clean teal focus style: 1px `#0F766E` border and `0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: 18x18px, 4px border radius. Unselected: 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border. Checked: `#0F766E` background with a white micro-check icon.
- Radio buttons: 18x18px round, selected with a 6px solid `#0F766E` center dot.

### Lists & Dispatch Tables
- Compact table layout with row height pinned to 48px.
- Subtle `#F8FAFC` zebra-striping or bottom-only `#E2E8F0` borders (0.5px).
- Numerical values, timestamps, and cargo tonnage align right; status tags and entity titles align left.

### Predictive Match Card (Specialized Platform Component)
- High-density rescue matching card featuring an automated AI Confidence Score (0–100%) formatted with a radial progress ring.
- Displays origin facility, recipient hub, expiry timer countdown, and an actionable "Approve Transfer" primary trigger button.
